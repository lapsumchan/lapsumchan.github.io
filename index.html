I am a biostatistician working on trustworthy biomedical AI and statistical methodology for modern biomedical data. My research connects calibrated risk prediction, survival analysis, and statistical genomics, with an emphasis on reliable conclusions and useful uncertainty assessments.

Research

My work addresses how complex biomedical data can support reliable prediction and inform decisions. Current interests include:

  • Calibrated risk prediction and uncertainty. Assessing the reliability of risk estimates and their implications for decisions.
  • Survival analysis and clinical histories. Statistical methods for time-to-event outcomes and longitudinal electronic health records.
  • Statistical genetics and genomics. Methods for association analysis, causal inference, and the study of complex traits.
  • High-dimensional and multimodal data. Methods that account for dependence and integrate information across biomedical data sources.

Additional research materials are available upon request.
